ABOUT ME

Curious about technology, focused on people

I am a Computer Science student at the University of Dundee, developing my skills as an aspiring software developer.

I am particularly interested in artificial intelligence, web development and software engineering. I enjoy working through challenging problems and creating practical, accessible experiences for the people who use them.

University projects have helped me build experience in programming and collaborative development. Working on a website redesign has also given me opportunities to contribute to planning, teamwork, client communication and accessible design.

Computing interests

I am keen to deepen my understanding of how software is designed and built. My current interests include AI and machine learning, web technologies, responsive design and making digital products easier to use.

Teamwork and communication

Collaborative project work has helped me practise sharing ideas, listening to feedback and working toward a common goal. I value clear communication and thoughtful planning throughout the development process.

Leadership and the outdoors

My experience as a climbing instructor has strengthened my communication, leadership and problem-solving skills. Helping people learn in an outdoor setting has taught me to adapt my approach, be patient and keep safety in mind.

How I approach learning

I enjoy taking on new challenges, learning from feedback and improving through practice. I aim to bring curiosity, reliability and a user-focused mindset to the software I build and the teams I work with.

My goals

I am continuing to grow as a developer by applying what I learn to practical projects and exploring the areas of computing that interest me most. I hope to contribute to software that is useful, well considered and built with its users in mind.
