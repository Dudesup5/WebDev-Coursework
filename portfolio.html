Ultimate Tic Tac Toe AI

In development

Artificial intelligence · Search algorithms

An ongoing project exploring how an AI can play Ultimate Tic Tac Toe. The work focuses on Monte Carlo Tree Search (MCTS) and neural network techniques to investigate how an agent can evaluate possible moves in a more complex version of the game.

My role

This is an individual project in progress. I am exploring the problem and investigating possible AI approaches rather than presenting it as a finished system.

Areas of focus

  • Applying search algorithms to game-playing problems.
  • Exploring MCTS and neural network approaches.
  • Building knowledge of Python and machine learning concepts.
  • Learning to break a complex decision problem into smaller steps.
